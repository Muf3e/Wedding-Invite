Mustafa & Tasneem — card design elements
==========================================
Plain SVG files, no text baked in. Open/drag them into Canva, Illustrator, Figma or
Inkscape and build your own card layout on top. Not linked from the wedding website.

COLORS (same palette as the site)
  Gold, main .......... #c9a24e
  Gold, light ......... #ecd9a0
  Gold, dark .......... #a8842f
  Lavender, main ...... #a58bdb
  Lavender, deep/text-purple #6e4fb3
  Ink (body text) ..... #5b5070
  Paper / ivory ....... #fffdf9

BACKGROUNDS (1000 x 1414 — an A-series portrait ratio; scale to whatever card size you pick)
  bg-parchment-border.svg   Ivory parchment + a fine gold/lavender texture + a gold
                            double-line border frame. The "safe" all-purpose background.
  bg-lavender-wash.svg      Softer lavender + gold watercolor wash, no border — a more
                            open canvas if you want to add your own frame/photo.

ELEMENTS
  element-ornament-divider.svg   Thin gold rule/divider — stretch the width as needed.
  element-corner-flourish.svg    Gold corner vine (drawn for top-left). Flip/rotate for
                                  the other three corners.
  element-monogram-frame.svg     Empty circular gold+lavender ring, transparent center —
                                  put your initials, a photo or your logo inside it.
  element-flower-bud.svg         One small 5-petal gold bud — duplicate/scatter for a
                                  sprig or a border of buds.
  element-arch-outline.svg       The site's ogee-arch outline (open at the bottom, like
                                  a doorway) — use as a frame or a photo mask.

All are vector, so resize freely without blur. Recolor by editing the stroke/fill hex
values above wherever they appear in each file.
